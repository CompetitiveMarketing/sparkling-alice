Our Touchless Approach

Snow foam.
No traditional hand wash.

Our touchless exterior washing process uses snow foam to help loosen dirt and grime before pressure rinsing.

By avoiding traditional hand washing of the paintwork, this process reduces physical contact with the vehicle's exterior.

It's a practical option for everyday vehicles, 4WDs and touring setups. Additional detailing treatments can be discussed separately.

  • Touchless snow foam exterior washing
  • No traditional hand washing of paintwork
  • Pressure rinsing to remove loosened grime
  • Suitable for a range of vehicle types
